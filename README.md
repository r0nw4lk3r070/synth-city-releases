# Synth City

**An all-in-one games app for Android.** Step into the Copper Cantina, a steampunk bar in a rainy cyberpunk city, and play your way through a war between the stars: cards at Tick-Tock's table, races through the Net, fleet battles, ground wars, starfighters and more. Alone, against the computer, or together over wifi.

## Download

👉 **[Download the latest version](https://github.com/r0nw4lk3r070/synth-city-releases/releases/latest)**: tap the `.apk` file under *Assets*.

1. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone.
2. If asked, allow installing apps from this source (your browser or file manager).
3. Done. From now on the app tells you itself when a new version is ready.

Updating works the same way: download the new `.apk` and open it. Your profiles and progress stay.

## Playing together

Everyone on the same wifi (or one phone's hotspot): open the notice board in the cantina → **LAN party**. One player hosts, the others join. No internet needed.
